# Contoso Living Maintenance Service Levels

Synthetic document for the Property Service Desk course project. Contoso Living is a fictional property manager in Dubai. This document sets out how quickly maintenance requests are answered and who pays for repairs.

## Response times

Every request is given an urgency when the ticket is raised.

- Emergency: a response within 2 hours. An emergency puts a person or the building at immediate risk, such as flooding or a smell of gas.
- Urgent: a response within 24 hours.
- Routine: a response within 5 working days.

The response time is the time until a technician or the property team first responds to the request, not the time until the repair is finished.

## Who pays for repairs

The property manager pays for normal wear and tear and for building systems.

The resident pays for damage they cause and for lost keys or cards.

## Emergencies

For an emergency, call the building's emergency line straight away, and raise a ticket as well.
