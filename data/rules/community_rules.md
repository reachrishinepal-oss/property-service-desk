# Contoso Living Community Rules

Synthetic document for the Property Service Desk course project. Contoso Living is a fictional property manager in Dubai. These rules apply to Contoso Heights A, Contoso Heights B and Contoso Gardens.

## Gym and pool

The gym is open every day from 05:30 to 23:00. Please wipe down equipment after use.

The pool is open from 07:00 to 21:00. The pool is closed on Mondays for cleaning. No glass is allowed in the pool area.

## Access cards

Each resident receives an access card for the lobby, lifts and shared facilities. A lost access card is replaced for a fee of AED 150, paid at the community office. Report a lost card to the community office as soon as possible so it can be blocked.

## Quiet hours and renovation

Quiet hours are from 22:00 to 07:00. Please keep music, television and conversation at a level that does not disturb neighbours during these hours.

Renovation noise, such as drilling or hammering, is allowed only from 09:00 to 17:00, Saturday to Thursday.

## Moving in and moving out

The move-in deposit is AED 2,000. It is refunded within 30 days after the move-out inspection.

## Visitor parking

Visitors may park for a maximum of 4 hours. Visitors must register at reception when they arrive.

## Emergencies

In an emergency, such as flooding, a smell of gas, smoke or fire, call the building's emergency line, or the emergency services if anyone is in danger.
