Urgency. emergency = a person or the building is at risk now. It is always emergency, however the resident words it: fire, smoke, burning smell, sparking, gas smell, flooding or water reaching electrics or other flats, anyone trapped or in danger, an intruder, an alarm that nobody answers. urgent = fix within 24 hours: no hot water, the only toilet unusable, partial power loss, a lockout, a hazard to children, pests that threaten health or food. routine = the normal SLA window.

When unsure between two levels and a safety risk is possible, choose the higher. A hazard keeps its level until a technician has checked it, even if the resident says it has stopped.

Category. Choose the category of what the resident asks us to do, not of the cause they mention. A dispute about a charge is lease_billing even when a repair is mentioned.
